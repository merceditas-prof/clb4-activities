Money and Banking

CLB 4 - YMCA LINC - Week 1: Canadian money, asking for change, bank accounts and bank statements

Day 1: Coins, Bills and Prices (2 h 30)

1 Look and Talk (10 min). Talk about the picture and match the money words.
2 Canadian Coins (25 min). The names and values of the coins, a short reading, and cash rounding.READINGCanada Revenue Agency - Phasing out of the penny (the rounding table)
Section 2E. Show the table on the screen: which cash totals go down and which go up?
3 Bills and Big Amounts (15 min). The colours of the bills; write big amounts in numbers and in words.SITEBank of Canada - Bank notes (the notes in our wallets today)
Section 3A. Click the $5 and the $20: look at the colours and the security features together.
4 Listening: Numbers and Prices (25 min). Thirteen or thirty? Listen and write prices (Tracks 1 and 2).VIDEORachel's English - How to Pronounce Numbers 20 - 1 Billion (7 min)
Section 4, before 4A. Show the part about teens and tens (thirteen - thirty); pause and students repeat.
5 How Much Is It? (20 min). Prices at a clothing store, a model conversation (Track 3), is / are, add the prices.
6 Count the Money (15 min). Count coins and bills; make an amount with three coins.
7 Making Change (25 min). Round a cash total, give the change, and a role play at the cash register.

Day 2: Asking for Change - How Much? How Many? (2 h 30)

1 Look and Talk (10 min). Talk about the picture and review Canadian coins and bills.
2 Asking for Change (25 min). Three conversations (Tracks 1-3) and the phrases to ask for change.
3 How Much? How Many? (25 min). Count and non-count nouns: money, coins, cash and change.GAMEWoodward English - How much vs How many (grammar game)
Section 3, finish. Play it on the projector; students call out the answers.
GAMETest-English - Much, many, a lot of, a little, a few (A1): explanation and exercise
Section 3 or homework. Exercise 1 has 10 questions: do the first five together.
READINGBritish Council LearnEnglish - Nouns: countable and uncountable (A1-A2)
Section 3, extra. Show the part about questions: how many and how much.
4 Counting Coins (20 min). Add coins and bills and say the total. Pair work with wallet cards.
5 Tipping in Canada (25 min). Read about tips in Canada and calculate a tip.READINGSun Life - How much should you tip? (a Canadian guide)
Section 5, extra. Show the list (restaurants, taxis, hair salons, delivery) and compare it with our text.
6 Speaking Practice: Ask for Change (30 min). Role plays in groups of three.

Day 3: Banks, Accounts and Account Plans (2 h 30)

1 Look and Talk (10 min). Talk about a picture of a bank and match what you can do at a bank.
2 Banks and Credit Unions (20 min). Read about banks, credit unions and deposit insurance.READINGFinancial Consumer Agency of Canada - Deposit insurance
Section 2, extra. Show what CDIC covers and the line about credit unions; read it together.
SITECDIC - What's covered
Section 2. Point to $100,000 and to free and automatic. Ask: what is NOT covered?
3 Bank Accounts (25 min). Chequing, savings and joint accounts: the words you need.
4 Opening an Account (25 min). Listen to Grace open an account (Track 1) and read about your rights.READINGFinancial Consumer Agency of Canada - Opening a bank account
Section 4D. The two ways to show ID, in the government's own words. Read the list of documents together.
5 Account Plans (35 min). Compare three account plans and find the cheapest one.SITEFCAC - Account Comparison Tool (real chequing and savings accounts)
Section 5, extra. Choose New Brunswick and compare the monthly fees of two real accounts.
LISTENINGISSofBC Learn English - Banking and Money (listening and reading by CLB level)
Section 5 or homework. CLB 3-5 listenings: Asking about fees, Minimum balance, Opening another account.
6 Interest (20 min). Who pays interest? Calculate the interest for one year.

Day 4: Reading a Bank Statement (2 h 30)

1 Look and Talk (10 min). Talk about the picture and review Day 3.
2 Statement Words and Codes (20 min). Match the words and the codes on a bank statement.GAMEESL Lounge - Banking words exercise (elementary, online)
Section 2, extra, or homework. A short online exercise with bank words; do the first items together on the screen.
3 The Parts of a Statement (20 min). Find the parts of Rosa's statement.
4 Read a Statement (30 min). Scan Rosa's statement and do small calculations.READINGAwalEnglish - Reading Activity 19: The Money Tracker (bank statements and balances)
Section 4, extra, or homework. A short reading about deposits, withdrawals and the closing balance, with a 6-question quiz.
5 Talk about a Statement (30 min). Listen to Ibrahim and Selam (Track 1) and practise Wh- questions.
6 Check Your Statement (25 min). Find three problems on Kwame's statement and call the credit union.READINGFinancial Consumer Agency of Canada - Resolving an unauthorized transaction
Section 6. Show the steps: notify your bank right away; you usually have 30 days after the statement date to dispute a transaction.
READINGFinancial Consumer Agency of Canada - Debit card fraud
Section 6, extra. Read the advice together: check your statements often and contact your bank immediately if something is wrong.
