Answer Key - Teacher Copy

13 points total. Do not show this tab when the screen is shared with the class.

Marking Question 11 (2 points). 1 point for the correct structure (could + base verb, or could try + verb-ing). 1 point for advice that is specific and fits the problem. Accept any sensible answer, for example: Sofia could try turning off the wifi at ten o'clock. / Sofia could buy an alarm clock so Ali does not need the phone in his room. / Sofia could talk to Ali about a bedtime. Do not take marks off for spelling.
Notes on tricky items. Q2 is False - Ali takes the phone to bed. Q5 has two correct answers; "That's a shame" and "Oh no! That's too bad" are not said in this conversation. Q7 "give in" means she stops saying no. Q8 "acts up" means he behaves badly.
