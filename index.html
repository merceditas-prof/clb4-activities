Email Guide

Study the structure of a good email to your landlord. Tap any phrase to copy it.

📌 Subject Line — Clear and specific

"Urgent: Leaking Faucet in Unit 2 — Immediate Attention Required"
Urgent: [Problem] in [Location] Follow-Up: [Problem] — No Response Request for Repair: [Problem] Safety Issue: [Problem] — Needs Immediate Attention

👋 Greeting

"Dear Mr./Ms. [Last Name],"
Dear Mr. [Name], Dear Ms. [Name], Hello Mr./Ms. [Name],

✉️ Reason for Writing — Explain the problem

"I am writing to inform you that there is a leaking faucet in my kitchen. The faucet has been dripping for three days."
I am writing to inform you that... I am contacting you regarding a problem in my apartment. I would like to bring to your attention that... I am a tenant at [address] and I am writing about... There is [a / an] ___ in my apartment. There are ___ in my apartment. The ___ has been [broken/leaking/not working] for [time].

⚠️ Urgency — Explain why it is important

"This is a safety concern, as water is damaging the floor. I am worried this could get worse."
This is a safety concern because... I am worried that this could cause further damage. This is affecting my daily life because... The situation is becoming worse. This is an urgent matter that requires immediate attention. I have a small child / elderly family member and this is a serious concern.

🙏 Request — Ask politely but clearly

"Could you please arrange for a plumber to come and fix the faucet as soon as possible?"
Could you please arrange for someone to fix this as soon as possible? I would appreciate it if you could address this issue promptly. Please let me know when someone will come to repair this. I kindly request that this be repaired at your earliest convenience. Could you please send someone to inspect the problem?

🔔 Follow-Up — When there has been no response

"I contacted you about this issue on [date] but have not yet received a response. I am following up to ask for an update."
I contacted you about this on [date] but have not received a response. I am following up on my previous message regarding... As I have not heard back from you, I am writing again to... I left a voicemail message on [date] about this issue. I would appreciate a response as soon as possible.

✅ Closing

"Thank you for your attention to this matter. I look forward to hearing from you soon.
Sincerely,
[Your Name]
Unit [#] — [Address]"
Thank you for your attention to this matter. I look forward to hearing from you soon. Thank you for your time. Sincerely, [Name] Best regards, [Name] Unit [#] — [Address] — [Phone]

Speaking Prep

Use this guide to plan your voicemail message to your landlord. Follow the 5 steps below.

📞 Voicemail Structure — 5 Steps
1

Greet and identify yourself

Say hello and say who you are and where you live.

"Hi, this is [your name]. I live in unit [#] at [address]."

"Hello, my name is ___ and I am a tenant at ___."

2

State the problem

Describe what is wrong. Use your vocabulary.

"I am calling because there is a problem in my apartment."

"I wanted to let you know that there is/there are ___."

"The ___ is broken / leaking / not working."

3

Explain the urgency

Say why it is important or how it affects you.

"This is a safety issue because ___."

"I am concerned because ___."

"This has been happening for ___ days / weeks."

4

Make your request

Ask politely for action.

"Could you please call me back to arrange a repair?"

"I would appreciate it if someone could come and fix this soon."

"Please call or text me at ___ when you get this message."

5

Leave your contact information and close

Give your phone number and say goodbye politely.

"My phone number is ___ . Thank you and I look forward to hearing from you."

"You can reach me at ___. Have a good day."

🗣️ Useful Phrases for Speaking

DESCRIBING THE PROBLEM

  • It is broken / not working
  • It has been leaking / dripping
  • The drain is clogged / blocked
  • The window is stuck
  • There is a loud noise
  • There are cockroaches / mice
  • It is very slippery / dangerous

MAKING A REQUEST

  • Could you please fix...?
  • I would appreciate it if...
  • Is it possible to send someone?
  • Please call me back at...
  • I would like to know when...
  • Could someone come and look at...?

Role Play Cards

Work in pairs. One person is the Tenant, one is the Landlord / Superintendent. Read your card only. Then switch roles!

📍 SITUATION

The kitchen faucet in the tenant's apartment has been dripping for five days. There is now a small water leak under the sink. The tenant called the landlord three days ago but got no response.

leakingdrippinga leaknot working

🏠 You are the Tenant

Your goal: Call the landlord / superintendent. Explain the problem and ask for urgent repair.

  • Introduce yourself and your unit
  • Describe the faucet problem
  • Mention the leak under the sink
  • Say you called before — no answer
  • Ask when someone can come to fix it

🔑 You are the Landlord

Your goal: Listen to the tenant and respond professionally.

  • Ask which unit they are in
  • Ask when the problem started
  • Ask: is it dripping or a big leak?
  • Apologize for the late response
  • Tell them you will send a plumber this week

📍 SITUATION

The tenant has seen cockroaches in the kitchen, near the stove and under the sink, for two weeks. The tenant is very concerned about hygiene and has a young child.

cockroachesthere arenot working

🏠 You are the Tenant

Your goal: Report the cockroach problem and ask for an exterminator.

  • Introduce yourself
  • Say there are cockroaches in the kitchen
  • Describe where you see them
  • Explain you have a young child — this is urgent
  • Ask the landlord to send an exterminator

🔑 You are the Landlord

Your goal: Take the complaint seriously and make a plan.

  • Thank them for letting you know
  • Ask: how many? Exactly where?
  • Ask: have other neighbours had this problem?
  • Say you will contact an exterminator this week
  • Ask them not to leave food out

📍 SITUATION

It is winter. The heater in the tenant's bedroom is not working. The temperature at night is very low. The tenant has been sleeping with extra blankets for one week and has emailed the landlord twice with no response.

brokennot workingblinking

🏠 You are the Tenant

Your goal: Demand urgent action — no heat in winter is a legal issue.

  • Introduce yourself and your unit
  • Say the heater is not working
  • Say you emailed twice — no response
  • Explain the urgency: it is cold at night
  • Ask for immediate repair or a temporary heater

🔑 You are the Landlord

Your goal: Respond urgently and professionally.

  • Apologize sincerely for not responding
  • Ask: Is it completely off or just not very warm?
  • Ask: Is the thermostat set correctly?
  • Say you will come today to check the furnace
  • Offer a temporary heater if needed

📍 SITUATION

The bathroom drain has been completely clogged for three days. Water is not going down and is pooling on the floor. The tenant cannot use the shower properly.

cloggedblockedpluggeda leak

🏠 You are the Tenant

Your goal: Report the clogged drain and request a plumber.

  • Introduce yourself
  • Say the bathroom drain is clogged
  • Explain water is pooling — you can't shower
  • Say it has been 3 days
  • Ask the landlord to send a plumber urgently

🔑 You are the Landlord

Your goal: Assess the situation and make a plan.

  • Ask: Is it slow or completely blocked?
  • Ask: Did you try a plunger?
  • Ask: Have you put any food or grease down the drain?
  • Say you will send a plumber tomorrow
  • Give a time window (morning/afternoon)

📍 SITUATION

Every night between 11pm and 2am there is a very loud banging noise coming from the pipes inside the bedroom wall. The tenant cannot sleep and has been tired for two weeks.

loudmaking a noisenot working

🏠 You are the Tenant

Your goal: Report the noise problem and ask for investigation.

  • Introduce yourself
  • Describe the noise: loud, from the pipes, every night
  • Say what time it happens
  • Explain it is affecting your sleep and daily life
  • Ask the landlord to investigate and fix the problem

🔑 You are the Landlord

Your goal: Take the complaint seriously and investigate.

  • Ask: What time does it happen?
  • Ask: Is it banging, hissing, or a different sound?
  • Ask: Has it gotten louder recently?
  • Say you will send a plumber to inspect the pipes
  • Thank them for letting you know
💡 How to do this activity
  1. Choose a scenario card above
  2. Student A = Tenant  |  Student B = Landlord
  3. Read your card only — do not show your partner
  4. Have the conversation (phone call or face to face)
  5. Switch roles and do it again
  6. After: together, decide — what was the best solution?
