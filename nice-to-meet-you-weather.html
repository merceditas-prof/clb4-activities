Nice to Meet You + How's the Weather?

CLB 4 | first class: getting to know each other, weather and temperature words, small talk | class page + practice games

First Class: Nice to Meet You + How's the Weather? (2 h 30, in person)

1. Welcome (10 min). Name cards. Two truths and a lie: the teacher first, then the students.
2. Before you start (10 min). Look at the classroom picture. Then match the questions and the answers.
3. Interview your partner (25 min). PAIRS. Put the questions in order, for or since, then the interview. Introduce your partner to the class (Audio tab, Track 1 is the model).
4. Find someone who (15 min). WHOLE CLASS. Make the questions, then stand up, walk around and ask. A different name in each box.
5. Our class (10 min). Class days and times, the class rules, and PBLA in simple words. Then a 10-minute break.
Break (10 min). Part 2 is about the weather.
6. Weather words (15 min). Words with pictures and sound on Learning Chocolate, then the unit document. Games: What's the weather? and sun or sunny?GAMETEACHER'S PICKLearning Chocolate - Weather (words with pictures and sound)
Section 6. Play the words, the class repeats. Then Match-up 1 together; the Dictation tab works as a spelling check.
7. The temperature (15 min). Watch the video and fill in the thermometer. Then listen and write the temperature (Audio tab, Track 3). Games: Hot or cold? and Listen: the temperature.VIDEOTEACHER'S PICKWoodward English - The Temperature (video)
Section 7. Once without stopping, then pause at each word: What temperature is hot? Students fill in the thermometer (7A).
SITEWoodward English - The Temperature (the chart from the video)
Section 7. The same words and temperatures on one page - show it to check 7A.
8. Small talk (15 min). Four short conversations (Audio tab, Tracks 4 to 7). Then practise small talk standing up, changing partners.
9. Communicate: Weather across Canada (20 min). GROUPS of four. Ask about the eight cities and complete the table. Then talk about the weather in your country.SITEEnvironment Canada - weather in Canadian cities right now
Section 9, after the cards. Real weather: What's the weather like in Toronto today? What's the temperature?
LISTENINGBritish Council - Weather forecast (A2 listening with exercises)
Extra or homework. Play the forecast; students do the matching and the gap-fill on the page.
Homework: the practice form (30 questions, you see your score at the end) - open the form - plus any game on this page.
