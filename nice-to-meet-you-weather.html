First Class: Nice to Meet You + How's the Weather? (2 h 30, in person)

1. Welcome (10 min). Name cards. Two truths and a lie: the teacher first, then the students.
2. Before you start (10 min). Look at the classroom picture. Then match the questions and the answers.
3. Interview your partner (25 min). PAIRS. Put the questions in order, for or since, then the interview. Introduce your partner to the class (Audio tab, Track 1 is the model).
4. Find someone who (15 min). WHOLE CLASS. Make the questions, then stand up, walk around and ask. A different name in each box.
5. Our class (10 min). Class days and times, the class rules, and PBLA in simple words. Then a 10-minute break.
Break (10 min). Part 2 is about the weather.
6. Weather words (15 min). Words with pictures and sound on Learning Chocolate, then the unit document. Games: What's the weather? and sun or sunny?GAMETEACHER'S PICKLearning Chocolate - Weather (words with pictures and sound)
Section 6. Play the words, the class repeats. Then Match-up 1 together; the Dictation tab works as a spelling check.
7. The temperature (15 min). Watch the video and fill in the thermometer. Then listen and write the temperature (Audio tab, Track 3). Games: Hot or cold? and Listen: the temperature.VIDEOTEACHER'S PICKWoodward English - The Temperature (video)
Section 7. Once without stopping, then pause at each word: What temperature is hot? Students fill in the thermometer (7A).
SITEWoodward English - The Temperature (the chart from the video)
Section 7. The same words and temperatures on one page - show it to check 7A.
8. Small talk (15 min). Four short conversations (Audio tab, Tracks 4 to 7). Then practise small talk standing up, changing partners.
9. Communicate: Weather across Canada (20 min). GROUPS of four. Ask about the eight cities and complete the table. Then talk about the weather in your country.SITEEnvironment Canada - weather in Canadian cities right now
Section 9, after the cards. Real weather: What's the weather like in Toronto today? What's the temperature?
LISTENINGBritish Council - Weather forecast (A2 listening with exercises)
Extra or homework. Play the forecast; students do the matching and the gap-fill on the page.
Homework: the practice form (30 questions, you see your score at the end) - open the form - plus any game on this page.

Getting the Weather Forecast - Lesson 1: The Long-Range Forecast

Four class days. All the forecasts on this page are practice forecasts for English class - they are not real forecasts.

Day 1: Weather in Canada and Forecast Words

1 Before You Start (10 min). The picture, how we check the weather, and a quick review of weather adjectives.
2 The Four Seasons (20 min). Read about the seasons. True or false, which season is it, and pair talk.
3 Five Regions of Canada (20 min). The map and the five regions. Compare the weather here with your country.READINGGovernment of Canada - Settling in Canada: land and climate
Section 3, extra. The four seasons and the five regions for newcomers. A bit harder than our text: read one season together.
Break (10 min).
4 What Kind of Forecast? (15 min). Forecast and report; short-range and long-range.
5 Forecast Words (25 min). The words and pictures on a weather website: showers, flurries, mainly sunny, wind chill ...GAMETEACHER'S PICKLearning Chocolate - Weather (words with pictures and sound)
Section 5, warm-up. Review last week's words; the Dictation tab is a spelling check.
GAMEGames to Learn English - Weather (match, map and sentences)
Section 5, finish. Play one round on the projector; students call out the answers.
VIDEOWoodward English - Weather Vocabulary in English (8 min)
Section 5 or homework. Nouns, adjectives and verbs: rain, rainy, it is raining. Pause at the chart.
6 Will and Won't (25 min). It will be sunny. It won't rain. There will be showers. Dictation (Track 1) and pair practice (Track 2).VIDEOWoodward English - WILL: how to use will in English (11 min)
Section 6. Show only the first part (the form: will, won't, questions). The rest is for homework.
VIDEOTeachers Mark and Matt - Predictions: will / won't (4 min)
Section 6, short option. Stop at 2:55 (after won't); the last part is about going to.
GAMETest-English - will (A1): explanation and three exercises
Section 6 or homework. Do exercise 1 together (10 questions); 2 and 3 at home.
7 Communicate: Our Predictions (20 min). Groups predict next Saturday's weather. Then we check the real forecast.SITEEnvironment Canada - Saint John, NB: 7-day forecast
Section 7C. The real forecast: point to Saturday and read it together. Who was right?
Homework, Day 1: the practice form (30 questions, you see your score at the end) - open the form - plus the Day 1 games on this page.

Day 2: The Parts of a Long-Range Forecast

1 Before You Start (10 min). Look at a 7-day forecast for Halifax. What can you see?VIDEOProfessor Hannah - How to Read a Weather Forecast (5 min, beginner)
Section 1 or homework. An easy reading of a forecast with is, was and will. Good for students who need more support.
2 Two Parts: Overview and Details (20 min). The general overview is at the top. The details are at the bottom.
3 A Forecast Is a Table (15 min). Titles, rows, columns and cells.
4 Short Forms and Numbers (25 min). POP, km/h, mm, cm, h. Listen and write the numbers (Track 1).VIDEOTEACHER'S PICKWoodward English - The Temperature (4 min)
Section 4, review. Saying temperatures: degrees, minus, below zero. You showed this one on October 5.
Break (10 min).
5 Scanning for Certain Days (25 min). Move your eyes quickly. Find only the days you need.
6 Communicate: Information Gap (25 min). Ask your partner for the missing numbers (Track 2 is the model).
7 A Real Forecast (15 min). Read the real 7-day forecast for our city.SITEThe Weather Network - Saint John, NB: 7 days
Section 7. The real forecast: students fill in the table for the next three days (feels like, POP, wind).
SITEThe Weather Network - Saint John, NB: 14 days
Section 7, extra. A real long-range forecast in a table: find the titles, a row, a column and the weekend.
SITETEACHER'S PICKThe Weather Channel - Saint John West: 10-day forecast
Section 7, extra. A third website with a different layout. Where is the POP on this one?
Homework, Day 2: the practice form (30 questions, you see your score at the end) - open the form - plus the Day 2 games on this page.

Day 3: Finding Details, Comparing Days, Making Decisions

1 Before You Start (10 min). A 7-day forecast for Alma, beside Fundy National Park.
2 Scanning for Details (25 min). The Z pattern: down the column, left to the name, right to the cell.
3 Comparing Days (25 min). Colder than, windier than, the hottest, the most rain. Which day is it? (Track 1)READINGWoodward English - Comparatives and Superlatives (rules and chart)
Section 3. Project the summary chart while students compare two days.
VIDEOWoodward English - Comparative and Superlative Adjectives (11 min)
Section 3 or homework. Show the rules, pausing for a weather example: colder, windier, the wettest.
GAMEWoodward English - Comparatives and Superlatives game (multiple choice)
Section 3, finish. A team quiz on the projector.
GAMETest-English - Comparative and superlative adjectives (A2)
Homework. Exercise 1 (10 questions). Skip the part about adverbs.
Break (10 min).
4 Winter Numbers (15 min). Below zero: minus fifteen is colder than minus eight.READINGGovernment of Canada - Wind chill and the Wind Chill Index
Section 4, extra. Read the first sentence together, then look at the table: at minus 30, what should you wear?
5 Making Decisions (20 min). Should, because, so: what will you do, and why?
6 Weather Alerts (10 min). Read a rainfall warning and a freezing rain warning.READINGGovernment of Canada - Weather alerts: warning, advisory, watch
Section 6. The kinds of alerts in plain words. A bit hard: show only the headings.
SITEEnvironment Canada - weather alerts map for New Brunswick
Section 6. Is there an alert for our area today?
7 Communicate: Plan a Getaway (30 min). Your group plans four days in Banff from the forecast (Track 2 is the model).SITEParks Canada - Banff National Park: activities and experiences
Section 7, extra. The real list of things to do in Banff: which ones are for winter?
Homework, Day 3: the practice form (30 questions, you see your score at the end) - open the form - plus the Day 3 games on this page.

Day 4: Review and Reading Skill-Using Task

1 Review (20 min). Forecast words, the parts of a forecast, and the grammar from this week.
2 Practice Task (25 min). Read a 7-day forecast for Winnipeg and answer twelve questions.LISTENINGAll Things Grammar - 7 Day Weather Forecast: easy English mini dialog (3 min)
Section 2, extra, or homework. A TV forecast for seven days. Students listen for the weather on each day.
LISTENINGBritish Council - Weather forecast (A2 listening with exercises)
Homework. It prepares the next lesson (listening to a weather report).
3 Report Your Plan (25 min). Each group reports its plan for Banff (Track 1 is the model).SITEParks Canada - Banff National Park: activities and experiences
Section 3. Show it while the groups report: did anyone choose something from the real list?
Break (10 min).
4 Write a Message (25 min). Write to a friend about the weather and your plans.
5 Reading Skill-Using Task (40 min). Your teacher will give you the task.
Homework, Day 4: play the Day 4 games on this page (Forecast - Games).
